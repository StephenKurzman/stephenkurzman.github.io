ABOUT
Technical depth that stays connected to the physical product.

My foundation is hands-on: soft materials, pattern development, sewing construction, foam, substrates, retention, and the physical details that determine whether a product can actually achieve its intended surface, feel, quality, and durability.

Over time, that technical depth expanded into broader product leadership across Design, Engineering, suppliers, Manufacturing, quality, cost, documentation, and production readiness. I work best where a team needs someone who can understand the physical problem, communicate it clearly across disciplines, and keep development moving without losing product intent.

Automotive has been the environment in which I built that discipline, but the underlying capability applies to any complex physical product where soft materials, engineered systems, human interaction, manufacturing, and design intent have to resolve together.
